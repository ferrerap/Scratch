## P2.4
- **A:** The thesis's strongest ground, mostly normative and analogical. Standard-setters require service-organisation reports to describe tests performed and results because user auditors need them to link to their own work; missing test descriptions trigger extra procedures; the AAI rule requires identification of data gaps; courts accept validated coverage evidence (recall estimates, sampling of the null set, disclosed protocol, adversarial challenge) in place of exhaustive manual review.
